statechart-viz

Draws any Fulcro statechart as a Graphviz diagram, with the active states highlighted.

Edit the code, Run, and click events to step the chart. Click a box in the drawing to zoom into it and a ↗ stub to step back out; scroll over it to magnify, drag to pan. Share copies a link that opens your chart.

Link to your own chart without opening the page first: add #src= and the raw URL of a .cljs file, e.g. a gist. More ways.

Your code runs in your browser with SCI (Scittle); nothing is sent to a server.
